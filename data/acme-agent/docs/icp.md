# Acme Corp — Ideal Customer Profile

Acme sells AI-native sales intelligence. ICP for outbound:

## Tier A (highest fit)

- 50–500 employees
- Series B–D venture backed
- Industries: fintech, healthtech, vertical SaaS, B2B AI tooling
- Revenue band: $5M–$80M ARR
- Sales-led GTM with at least 5 SDRs/AEs
- North America or UK

## Tier B (mid fit)

- 20–50 OR 500–2000 employees
- Pre-Series-B with strong product traction OR public companies
- Industries: hybrid SaaS/services, healthcare-adjacent, regulated industries
- Anywhere in NA, UK, Western EU

## Tier C (low fit)

- <20 employees, pre-revenue, hobbyist
- Pure services firms, agencies, consultancies
- E-commerce DTC brands
- Industries we have not historically converted: gaming, gambling, crypto, defense

## Hard disqualifiers

- Stealth-mode / no website
- No GTM motion (research-only)
- Listed on sanctions filter

## Signals we track

- LinkedIn employee count growth (last 90 days)
- Open SDR/AE roles
- Recent funding events
- Job posts mentioning AI tooling
